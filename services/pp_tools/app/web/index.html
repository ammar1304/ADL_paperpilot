    How it works

    An automation workflow runs each step in a fixed order. Language models do the reading and judging; code checks everything they answer before you see it.

    Novelty

    The idea is split into task, method, data and result. Several searches run over the library, fused and reranked. A search agent decides whether to look again, here or on the web. A judge compares the idea with each paper, and code rejects any paper or passage the search never returned.

    Draft audit

    The PDF is parsed into text, tables and figure images. Claims are extracted from the sentences that carry numbers, figures or citations. Numbers are recomputed from the table cells; figures are read by a vision model; citations are checked against passages retrieved from the cited paper.

    What a verdict means

    "Supported" and "contradicted" always come with the evidence. "Unverifiable" or "not found" means the evidence was missing, not that the claim is wrong. Saying a claim is wrong needs more evidence than saying it is right.
